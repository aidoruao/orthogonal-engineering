4. Flawless Recoil Decoupling

+
Differential Equations Calculus Mechanical Materials Vibration Damping

Maximum negative recoil creates a violent, oscillating muzzle climb. Your muscle memory must apply a mirrors-inverted, continuous counter-pressure that exactly negates the weapon's unique mathematical bounce pattern. This is a damped harmonic oscillator: mẍ + cẋ + kx = F(t). Mechanical engineers solve this for engine mounts and aircraft wings. You solve it in real-time with your thumb.

Math core: Recoil pattern y(t) = A·e^(-ζωt)·sin(ω√(1-ζ²)t). Counter-input must be y_c(t) = -y(t) with phase lag under 16ms (one frame at 60Hz).
