Let $V \circ F: L \to \text{Bool}$ be a semantic property of the constructions. Rice's Theorem states that any non-trivial semantic property of a partial recursive function space is undecidable.

$$\neg \text{RE}(L) \vee \neg \text{ContainsPartialRecursiveFunctions}(C)$$

* **Case 1:** If $C$ contains all partial recursive functions and $F(L)$ maps to indices of these functions, then $V \circ F$ is uncomputable.
