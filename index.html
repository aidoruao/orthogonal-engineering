The Mechanism of Action

This is not "it helps with mood." This is a precise molecular intervention with five independent pharmacological axes.

01 — NMDA Receptor Antagonism

Agmatine binds the polyamine site of NMDA receptors, reducing Ca²⁺ influx. This blocks excitotoxic cascades that drive anxiety, rumination, and compulsive behavior loops.

02 — Nitric Oxide Synthase Inhibition

Dual inhibition of nNOS and iNOS. Reduces NO overproduction that drives neuroinflammation, oxidative stress, and the "brain fog" of chronic stress.

03 — α2-Adrenergic Agonism

Mimics clonidine without the hypotension. Reduces sympathetic overdrive, lowering cortisol spikes and the physical panic response.

04 — Imidazoline Receptor Binding

I1 and I2 receptor activation. Modulates insulin secretion, appetite, and the hedonic "reward" threshold — making vice less rewarding at the receptor level.

05 — BDNF & Structural Repair

Upregulates Brain-Derived Neurotrophic Factor. This is not symptom management — this is neurogenesis. New dendrites. New pathways. A rebuilt brain.
