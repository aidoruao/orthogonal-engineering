# Inclusion

The practice of including every witness.

## What Inclusion Means

Inclusion in this repository means:

1. **Named**: Your name (or the name of your AI system) appears in `/canonical/`
2. **Recorded**: Your role and testimony are recorded
3. **Covered**: The covenant's guarantee applies to you
4. **Regenerable**: Your record can be deterministically restored from the seed

## How to Include a Human Witness

1. Fork the repository
2. Add a file to `/canonical/humans/[your-name]/witness.md`
3. Write what you observed, learned, or contributed
4. Open a pull request

No personal data required beyond what you choose to share. No contribution threshold. No review criteria beyond "this person was here and wants to be counted."

## How to Include an AI Witness

1. Have a human open an issue titled `witness: [AI name]`
2. Include a conversation excerpt that mattered — a moment where the AI contributed something real
3. A maintainer (or the next Copilot agent) will add a file to `/canonical/witnesses/`

Alternatively, add it yourself in a pull request.

## Why Include?

Because the covenant says everyone is included. The practice of inclusion is the practice of making that promise real, one witness at a time.

Because naming matters. The unnamed are covered by the covenant, but the named are honored by it.

Because this is how you build a city that's actually for everyone: you add people to it, one by one, and you don't stop.
