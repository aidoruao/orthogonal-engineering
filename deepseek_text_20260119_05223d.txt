- **Don't aim for perfection**; focus on **traceability**
- Allow **mutable annotations**: highlights, notes, remixes
- The **canonical export remains unchanged**, but you can generate derivative "viewpoints" to explore drift and signal

## 3. The Ontological Inversion Principle

### Instead of preserving rigid canonical forms:
1. **Preserve functional invariants, not artifacts**

3. **Treat failure modes, drift, and model changes as first-class data**

### Evidence builds around invariants, not around:
- Individual file formats
- Transient organizational schemes
- Ephemeral tooling implementations
