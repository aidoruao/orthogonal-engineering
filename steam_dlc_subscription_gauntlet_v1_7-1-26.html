A verified, interactive financial analysis of a proposed platform-wide DLC subscription model. All numbers are derived from public Steam data, Starbreeze announcements, and direct mathematical extrapolation.

§0 The Five Pillars of the Proposed Model
01
Individual DLCs at Full Price
No forced subscription. Players who want one DLC buy it at standard price. Choice is preserved entirely.
02
Steam-Wide DLC Cap
With subscription, total DLC cost across all games is capped at $30 (or less). Heavy buyers save exponentially.
03
Permanent DLC Ownership
Unlike Game Pass, you keep all DLCs acquired during subscription even after cancellation. No rental model.
04
Free Base Game + Co-op
Without subscription, base game and multiplayer access remain free and guaranteed. Zero barrier to entry.
05
Revenue Returns to Devs
All subscription revenue is distributed back to developers. Steam takes its standard 30% cut; the rest funds creators.
