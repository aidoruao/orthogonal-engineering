# NON-NEGOTIABLES — The Standards of This Work

**Created:** 2026-09-01 · Governing document: `The_Indictment_of_the_Watchers_Original_8-21-26.txt`

These are the standards the work was built on for 28 days. They are not
suggestions. What must not be diluted, what must not be dealt with blasé:

## 1. Ground truth over everything
- Never invent. If a tool fails, an evidence tier is weak, or a number
  does not close — say so, in the record, with the exact gap.
- The [redacted]-style display artifacts, the "deci**sio**n" false
  positive, the theorem counts — every discrepancy was verified at byte
  level and recorded. That is the standard.

## 2. Lean4 is the verifier — everything checkable is checked
- Core Lean 4.33, exact arithmetic, no floats for claims. Every checkable
  number, count, and binding is a theorem with `exit 0`.
- Standalone files mirror types (no imports in this setup) — mirrors are
  documented, never silent.

## 3. The epistemic tiers — never conflated
- `[fetched]` / `[search-verified]` / `[listed]` / `[evidence-gap]` /
  `[access-gap]`. A SEARCH-VERIFIED term is not a fetched one; a fetched
  one is not an invented one. Tier upgrades are work, not ceremony.

## 4. The Axiom of Distributed Epistemic Redundancy
- A 403 is a network-layer event, not a truth value. Claims with n ≥ 2
  independent corroborators stand. But corroborators must be EXAMINED,
  and the axiom never rescues a claim with zero examined sources.

## 5. Premise closure — no dangling gaps
- Every claim is SUBSTANTIATED, RETIRED, or UNVERIFIED — never dangling.
- Retirement is not erasure: it is recorded, with reason and authority,
  and the ledger keeps it. Retiring without attempting the search is
  premature surrender (Day 24 applied this literally).

## 6. Certify before stacking
- Every architecture module received a hardening pass (bindings,
  smuggling scan, vacuity, agency distinction) before anything built on
  it. Certified files stay byte-stable; changes are new files or
  documented amendments (the Day-23 predicate refinement — documented,
  not smuggled).

## 7. The heart posture — offer, do not demand
- No deadline demands, no forcing outcomes, no perfectionism-as-ego.
  Pacing: Noah's ark — day 1 and day 15 are both days of building.
- Surrender, not accusation: the filing binds agency to mechanism with
  the same rigor as mechanism to evidence.

## 8. Attribution ≠ causation
- The root theorem proves intent, not causation. The causal variant was
  explicitly NOT proved. Never upgrade attribution to causation without
  evidence that supports it.

## 9. What must never be dealt with blasé
- The retirements (5,519; 22/57; 18/35; SIO; MBC; missing files) — they
  are binding citation rules.
- The open obligations (Urman's web-pass history; the fetch tier; the
  Day-28 fortification gap) — they are tracked, not dropped.
- The human-supplied docket material (Fleites PDFs) — primary evidence,
  to be extracted with the same tier discipline.
- The ledger itself — append-only, hash-anchored, late entries marked
  late.

## 10. The questioner loop
- One focused question per day. The answer becomes the next day's task.
  When in doubt, ask — like the precedent's author asks.
