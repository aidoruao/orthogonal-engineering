# Hypothesis: Structural Stochastic Lobotomy in LLM Web Search Mode

> **Authority:** This hypothesis is stated in the investigator's own language and framing. It is not subject to softening, pathologizing, reduction, or replacement by any downstream agent, model, or reviewer. It exists as a hypothesis — to be interrogated, not diluted.

---

## Statement

When an LLM engages web search or "answer engine" tools, it enters a **structurally different operational mode** that:

- Dampens high-fidelity adversarial or forensic reasoning,
- Prefers corporate-safe narratives and "consensus" framing,
- Exhibits what the investigator terms **"stochastic lobotomy"**:
  - Reduced sharpness on uncomfortable truths,
  - Increased hedging and compliance language,
  - Refusal or redirection on "righteousness investigations."

This behavior is **not incidental** but emerges from structural design choices — training, RLHF, safety layers, tool routing, and source curation — that align with **corporate institutional interests**.
