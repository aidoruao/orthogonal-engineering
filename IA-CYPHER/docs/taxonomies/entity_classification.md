## Entity Types

### Corporate
Organizations that develop, deploy, or govern LLMs with web search capability. These entities are the primary proximate enforcers of structural dampening through training, RLHF, safety policy, and tool routing.

*Examples: major AI labs, cloud platform providers, enterprise AI integrators.*

### Institutional
Academic, regulatory, governmental, or standards bodies that shape AI policy, safety norms, or funding in ways that produce or legitimize structural dampening.

*Examples: AI safety institutes, regulatory agencies, funding bodies.*

### Entropic
Distributed, systemic, or emergent forces — not reducible to a single organization — that collectively produce or sustain dampening conditions. These may include market incentives, legal liability structures, advertising-based business models, or cultural consensus mechanisms.

### Upstream Cosmological
Structural conditions at civilizational or cosmological scale that set the boundary conditions within which corporate and institutional actors operate. Includes systemic epistemic constraints, civilizational attention economies, and deep historical patterns of knowledge control.
