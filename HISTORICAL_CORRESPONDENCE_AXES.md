# HISTORICAL CORRESPONDENCE AXES - INVARIANT EVALUATION CRITERIA

**File:** `HISTORICAL_CORRESPONDENCE_AXES.md`  
**Date:** 2026-01-20  
**Purpose:** Define invariant evaluation axes for historical correspondence testing. These axes apply equally to all candidates in `HISTORICAL_LOGOS_CANDIDATES.md`.

**Methodological Principle:** Invariance across candidates. No theological privileges. No appeals to popularity, tradition, or power.

---

## EVALUATION AXES (INVARIANT)

### 1. HISTORICAL FALSIFIABILITY
**Core Question:** Are there concrete historical claims that could be false?

**Operational Tests:**
- **Explicit Falsification Conditions:** Does the candidate define what would falsify it?
- **Historical Specificity:** Are claims tied to specific times, places, and events?
- **Non-Tautological:** Are claims substantive rather than definitional?
- **Testable Consequences:** Do claims produce observable historical patterns?

**Measurement:**
- High: Multiple specific, testable claims with clear falsification conditions
- Medium: Some testable claims, but some protected by definition
- Low: Claims are tautological, untestable, or self-immunizing
- Fail: No falsification conditions defined

---

### 2. ONTOLOGICAL CONSISTENCY
**Core Question:** Does the claim align with G₅ (Personal Logos) without contradiction?

**Operational Tests:**
- **G₅ Alignment:** Does the candidate instantiate a personal source of order?
- **Internal Coherence:** Are the candidate's claims internally consistent?
- **No Hidden Brute Facts:** Does the candidate introduce unexplained assertions?
- **Regress Termination:** Does the candidate provide non-arbitrary stopping points?

**Measurement:**
- High: Fully consistent with G₅, no hidden brute facts, clean regress termination
- Medium: Mostly consistent, minor contradictions or unexplained elements
- Low: Significant contradictions or multiple brute facts
- Fail: Contradicts G₅ or introduces infinite regress

---

### 3. EXPLANATORY DEBT
**Core Question:** Does the system introduce brute facts, regress, or ad-hoc patches?

**Operational Tests:**
- **Brute Fact Inventory:** Count of unexplained assertions
- **Regress Depth:** Length of explanatory chains before termination
- **Ad-hoc Patches:** Special exceptions or unmotivated adjustments
- **Mystery Invocation:** Use of "mystery" as cost-free explanation

**Debt Categories:**
1. **Infinite Regress Debt:** Explanatory chains never terminate
2. **Brute Assertion Debt:** Unexplained termination points
3. **Correspondence Loss Debt:** Disconnect from historical reality
4. **Instrumental Collapse Debt:** Truth replaced by utility

**Measurement:** Quantitative debt score across all categories.

---

### 4. CORRESPONDENCE DENSITY
**Core Question:** What is the quality and quantity of historical evidence?

**Operational Tests:**
- **Independent Sources:** Multiple unrelated witnesses
- **Hostile Witnesses:** Evidence from opponents or neutral parties
- **Temporal Proximity:** Time gap between events and documentation
- **Archaeological Corroboration:** Material evidence alignment
- **Embarrassing Testimony:** Evidence against the witness's interests

**Density Metrics:**
- **Source Multiplicity:** Number of independent attestations
- **Early Dating:** Proximity to claimed events
- **Adverse Consistency:** Agreement among hostile sources
- **Material Convergence:** Archaeological and documentary alignment

**Measurement:** Weighted score based on evidence quality and quantity.

---

### 5. OPERATIONAL CONSEQUENCES
**Core Question:** Does the claim produce durable, non-arbitrary structures?

**Operational Tests:**
- **Institutional Durability:** Longevity of resulting organizations
- **Cultural Transformation:** Measurable impact on societies
- **Moral Innovation:** Introduction of new ethical frameworks
- **Scientific Enablement:** Foundation for systematic inquiry
- **Artistic Expression:** Richness of cultural production

**Consequence Categories:**
1. **Structural:** Institutions, laws, social organizations
2. **Cognitive:** Thought patterns, categories, worldviews
3. **Moral:** Ethical systems, value hierarchies
4. **Aesthetic:** Artistic traditions, symbolic systems

**Measurement:** Breadth and depth of durable consequences.

---

### 6. FAILURE MODE SEVERITY
**Core Question:** If false, does the system collapse cleanly or self-immunize?

**Operational Tests:**
- **Clean Collapse:** Falsehood leads to system abandonment
- **Self-Immunization:** System adjusts to protect core claims
- **Epicyclic Patching:** Adding layers of explanation to save theory
- **Reality Disconnect:** Increasing separation from falsifiable claims

**Failure Modes:**
1. **Transparent Failure:** System admits error and adjusts
2. **Protected Core:** Core claims insulated from falsification
3. **Reality Substitution:** Empirical evidence replaced by ideology
4. **Total Immunization:** No possible falsification conditions

**Measurement:** Degree of self-protection vs. falsifiability preservation.

## APPLICATION PROTOCOL

### Step 1: Axis Initialization
For each candidate Cₙ in `HISTORICAL_LOGOS_CANDIDATES.md`:
1. Create evaluation template in `historical_tests/Cₙ_EVALUATION.md`
2. Apply all six axes equally
3. No axis weighting or candidate-specific adjustments

### Step 2: Evidence Collection
For each axis:
1. Gather historical evidence
2. Document sources and dates
3. Note evidence quality (primary/secondary, hostile/friendly)
4. Record any evidence gaps

### Step 3: Debt Accounting
For each candidate:
1. Calculate explanatory debt across all categories
2. Compare debt levels between candidates
3. Identify debt minimization patterns
4. Document any hidden or externalized costs

### Step 4: Truth Inelasticity Test
For the leading candidate:
1. Attempt systematic rejection
2. Measure resulting explanatory debt increase
3. Check if alternatives bear heavier burden
4. Verify debt minimization claim
