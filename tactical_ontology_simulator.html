Engagement Geometry

Slicing the Pie (RoN): Angular sector reduction via iterative observation. Each slice reveals a fraction Δθ of the threat space while maintaining cover fraction κ behind the wall.

Fatal Funnel Breach (CoD): Rapid entry through doorway threshold τ. Exposure time E = w/v where w = doorway width, v = entry velocity. Momentum p = mv with gear mass m ∈ [30,45] kg under physics fidelity φ.
P(hit|exposed) = 1 − exp(−λ·E)
λ = (accuracy × visibility × 1/φ)

Newtonian Kinematics (Entry Dynamics)

v_max = √(2·a_max·d_entry) · (1 − φ·m_gear/m_max)
a_max = μ·g · (1 − 0.4·φ) [friction penalty under load]
KE = ½·m·v² [unrecoverable during slide]
t_exposure = w_doorway / v_entry
Under maximal physics fidelity (φ→1), the CoD operator cannot instantaneously change direction. Momentum conservation dictates that a 75 kg operator + 35 kg kit at 4 m/s carries 440 kg·m/s of linear momentum. Redirecting this requires impulse ∫F·dt, which in a doorway fatal funnel translates to predictable ballistic trajectories for the defender.

Ballistic Trajectory & Terminal Ballistics

Aerospace parallel: Treating the projectile as a body with drag coefficient C_d, cross-section A, mass m. The defender's "engagement window" is analogous to a launch intercept envelope—time is the only irreplaceable resource.

Interior Ballistics — Energy Transfer Model

E_transfer = ½·m_proj·(v_impact² − v_exit²) = ΔE_tissue
Penetration depth δ = (m_proj / A) · (v_impact² / 2·σ_yield) · cos(θ_deflection)
P(incapacitation) = 1 − exp(−k·ΔE_tissue / E_threshold)
The CoD-style "jump peek" exposes the center of mass vertically, increasing the presented cross-sectional area A_presented by ~18% compared to a crouched, pie-slicing posture. Under the ballistic model, this directly increases P(hit) independent of reaction time.

Nash Equilibrium & Mixed Strategy

Let p = P(CoD rushes), q = P(RoN holds)
E[U_CoD|hold] = 0.35·q + 0.55·(1−q)
E[U_CoD|flank] = 0.25·q + 0.60·(1−q)
Indifference: 0.35q + 0.55(1−q) = 0.25q + 0.60(1−q)
→ q* = 0.20, p* = 0.33
The Nash equilibrium demands the RoN player hold/pie only 20% of the time and breach aggressively 80% of the time to remain unexploitable. This reveals a counter-intuitive result: optimal RoN play is not pure caution—it is calibrated, unpredictable violence of action when the geometry favors it.

Extensive Form — Sequential CQB with Imperfect Information

Information sets: The defender (RoN) does not know if the attacker (CoD) has committed to a slide, a jump-peek, or a pre-fire. The attacker does not know if the defender is deep-holding or shallow-holding. This creates a Bayesian game where beliefs are updated as noise (footsteps, gear rattle) enters the signal channel.

Signaling: A deliberate footstep (RoN) is a costly signal of presence—intended to freeze the attacker. A sprint-slide (CoD) is a cheap signal that reveals position but compresses reaction time. Under the Spence signaling model, only the RoN player can afford costly signals because their survival is not contingent on surprise.

Yerkes-Dodson Stress-Performance Curve

Performance P(σ) = A·σ·exp(−σ/τ) where σ = stress/arousal, τ = optimal apex. CoD players operate at higher σ (time pressure, speed), pushing them right of apex. RoN players operate left-of-apex, preserving executive function for shoot/no-shoot discrimination.

OODA Loop Phase Timing (ms)

RoN: Extended Observe with Bayesian threat updating. Orient includes spatial mapping of cover sectors. Decide is multi-branch (comply/flash/engage/hold). Act is deliberate, minimizing sympathetic muscle contraction.

w_i = { threat_discrimination: 2.4, spatial_mapping: 1.8, comm_relay: 1.2, motor_control: 0.6 }

The CoD player's lower initial load spikes catastrophically when the pattern breaks—this is the inversion predicted by the model.
