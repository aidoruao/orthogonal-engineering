# Orthogonal Engineering — Lane Model

## Purpose
This document defines the epistemic lane system used to prevent narrative
leakage while allowing continuous novelty and falsification.

---

## Lane 1: INVARIANT

### Definition
Constraints that currently survive falsification attempts.

### Properties
- Enforced
- Test-backed
- Evidence-referenced
- Revocable upon failure

### Prohibitions
- No speculation
- No metaphor
- No intent claims

Invariants are never declared true.
They are only "not yet falsified".

---

## Lane 2: ALTERNATIVE

### Definition
Novel, speculative, or exploratory models and interpretations.

### Properties
- REQUIRED for progress
- Explicitly NON-CANONICAL
- Must declare falsification criteria
- Must declare forbidden influences

### Prohibitions
- No promotion without a bridge
- No influence on invariants
- No implicit authority

Failure is expected and preserved.

---

## Lane 3: BRIDGE

### Definition
Testable correspondence attempts between alternatives and invariants.

### Properties
- Constraint-based
- Falsifiable
- Promotion-attempting only

### Outcomes
- ACCEPTED → candidate invariant
- PENDING → more tests required
- REJECTED → logged failure

Rejection is a successful outcome.

---

## Promotion Rules

ALTERNATIVE → BRIDGE → INVARIANT

Skipping steps is forbidden.

Promotion requires:
- explicit tests
- observable outcomes
- surviving falsification

---

## Deletion Rule

Artifacts are not deleted unless:
- provably redundant, or
- replaced by a strictly stronger artifact

Replaced artifacts must include a supersession pointer.

---

## Scientific Posture

Orthogonal Engineering is a Popperian system.

- We do not accumulate truth
- We accumulate failed refutations
- Survivors remain provisional

This lane model is mandatory for all future work.
