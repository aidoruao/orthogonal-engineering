### 1. **Incarnation**: Lossy by choice — voluntary contamination
- Not informational preservation
- Christ limits divine prerogatives (not nature)
- Genuine human vulnerability experienced

### 2. **Substitution**: Forensic & particular — not abstract integration
- Each person's sin has specific bearer: Jesus Christ at Calvary
- Not abstract integration over chaos
- Particular, named, covenantal substitution

### 3. **Restoration**: Volitional love — not geometric distance
- Father runs the distance (Luke 15:20)
- Not geometric distance minimization
- Covenantal love (hesed) as mechanism

### 4. **Grace**: Debt erasure — not reduction
- τετέλεσται — paid in full (John 19:30)
- Infinite debt → 0 (not infinite debt → small debt)
- Complete erasure, standing ≡ "never sinned"

### 5. **Resurrection**: Generative new creation — exceeds restoration
- ℜ: ℳ_fallen → ℳ_new, where ℳ_new ⊋ ℳ_pre-fall
- Not rollback, but upgrade
- Generates new possibilities beyond original state

### 6. **Kenotic Override**: Love > Law when law condemns
- κ(rule) = MERCY if rule → death, else rule
- System stability < Salvation of person
- Priority interrupt for mercy

### 7. **Paradox Living**: Sustained paradox — not resolved
- Hypostatic union maintained without reduction
- Mystery acknowledged, not eliminated
- Christological paradoxes sustained eternally
