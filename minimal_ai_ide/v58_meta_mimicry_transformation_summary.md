it transforms **meta-mimicry** (the performance of appearing correct without substance)

Where previous systems (like V57) might engage in meta-mimicry by appearing to implement

### The V58 Self-Criticism Engine:
```

1. Consistency Checks: Does output contradict previous outputs?
2. Specification Checks: Does output meet unstated requirements?
3. Simplification Checks: Can output be simplified (Occam's razor)?
```
