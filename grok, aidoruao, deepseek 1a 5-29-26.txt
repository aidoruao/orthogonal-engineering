## II. How to modularize it as a LEGO set (for any usage)

The existing LEGO book series is your starting template. To make it a **universal, modular system** for any usage, you don't just build the LEGO set; you create the **grammar of building**:

### 1. The Physical Modules (Bricks)
You use physical LEGO bricks to represent the core concepts:

*   **Objects (A, B, C...):** Different colored 2x4 brick bases.
*   **Arrows (morphisms f: A → B):** Specific LEGO pieces (e.g., a transparent 1x4 brick) that can *connect* to the base bricks. Crucially, an arrow *must* be able to connect to the objects to be valid.
*   **Composition (g ∘ f):** Two arrow bricks placed end-to-end. The rule (associativity) is a physical constraint: if `f` connects A to B, and `g` connects B to C, the `g∘f` brick must be the *only* possible way to connect A to C.
*   **The Hom-Set:** A bag of all possible arrow bricks between two objects.
*   **The Hom-Functor (`Hom(A, -)`):** A *perspective*. To see the world from A's point of view, you look at all the arrow bricks *leaving* A and going to every other object. You are, in a sense, holding object A and looking out.

### 2. The Modular Operation (The Yoneda "Swap")
The core operation of your LEGO system is a physical, reversible transformation that embodies the Yoneda Lemma. You can build a device that does this, but even better, you can *act it out*:

1.  **Pick a target:** A specific LEGO object in your construction, let's call it `Secret_X`.
2.  **Map its relationships:** Draw a map (or create a new LEGO structure) that shows `Secret_X` connected to *every other* object by every possible arrow.
3.  **The Swap:** Remove `Secret_X`. The *map of its relationships* is now a freestanding object.
4.  **The Lemma:** Any other object that has the *exact same relationship map* to the rest of the world is, for all intents and purposes, identical to `Secret_X`. You can swap them and no one can tell the difference.

The Yoneda Lemma is the **rule that this swap is always valid**. The object's identity *is* its relationship map. There is no "secret hidden essence" inside the brick other than how it connects.
