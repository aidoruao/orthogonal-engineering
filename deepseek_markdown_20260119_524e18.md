# 🧭 Ontological Principles of Orthogonal Engineering

## 🎯 Core Philosophical Foundation

> **Ontological Inversion Principle**: Preserve functional invariants, not artifacts.  
> Scripts, formats, and chat exports are ephemeral vessels—the signal through structure is what survives.

---

## 1. The One Thing You Never Lose

**The mapping between structure and invariants.**

No matter how chaotic iterative work becomes, what must persist is the link between:
- **Content** (the raw conversation)
- **Structural form** (how it's organized)
- **Extractable meaning** (verified invariants)

**Markdown files, JSON, Python scripts, chat exports**—these are all *vehicles*.  
The **structural invariants themselves** are the permanent signal.  
Everything else (drift, formatting, ephemeral scripts) is auxiliary.

---

## 2. Maximal Handling of Chat Exports

### Treat Chat Exports as Canals Themselves

- Each verified invariant → a **potential extraction target**
- Windows of interaction (5-turn or flexible) → **define local structure**
