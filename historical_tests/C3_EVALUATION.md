## CLAIM SUMMARY

**Core Claim:** Muhammad is the final prophet through whom the Logos (Allah) communicates definitively to humanity.

**Historical Assertion:** A 7th-century Arab merchant from Mecca received final revelation from the personal source of reality.

**Key Implications:**
- Allah (Arabic for God) is the personal source of reality
- Quran is the literal, perfect word of Allah revealed to Muhammad
- Muhammad's life (Sunnah) provides perfect example for humanity
- Islam provides the complete and final revelation

---

## EXPLICIT ASSUMPTIONS

1. **Historical Existence:** Muhammad existed as a historical figure in 7th-century Arabia
2. **Prophetic Claim:** Muhammad claimed and was accepted as a prophet
3. **Divine Revelation:** Quran was revealed by Allah through Gabriel
4. **Inerrancy:** Quran is perfect and without error
5. **Finality:** Muhammad is the final prophet, no further revelation needed

---

## PRIMARY HISTORICAL ASSERTIONS

1. **Existence:** Muhammad lived c. 570-632 CE in Arabia
2. **Revelation:** Received revelations from 610 CE until death
3. **Migration:** Led Hijra from Mecca to Medina in 622 CE
4. **Political Leadership:** Established Islamic state in Medina
5. **Military Campaigns:** Led military expeditions and conquered Mecca
6. **Textual Preservation:** Quran preserved perfectly from revelation
7. **Exemplary Life:** Muhammad's life provides perfect moral example

## WHAT WOULD FALSIFY THIS

### Direct Falsification:
1. **Non-Existence:** Proof Muhammad never existed historically
2. **Contradictory Revelation:** Quran contains errors or contradictions
3. **Moral Failure:** Muhammad's actions contradict perfect moral example
4. **Historical Impossibility:** Events described couldn't have occurred
5. **Textual Corruption:** Proof Quran was altered or not preserved

### Indirect Falsification:
1. **Better Explanation:** Alternative account better explains all historical data
2. **Lower Debt Alternative:** Another candidate has strictly lower explanatory debt
3. **Internal Inconsistency:** Islamic theology contains irreconcilable contradictions
4. **Failed Predictions:** Islamic claims about reality consistently fail
5. **Moral Failure:** Islamic practice produces worse outcomes than alternatives

### Supporting Evidence:
1. **Early Biographies:** Ibn Ishaq and other early biographies
2. **Hadith Collections:** Extensive traditions about Muhammad's life
3. **Documentary Evidence:** Early Quranic manuscripts
4. **Rapid Expansion:** Islamic empire expanded rapidly after Muhammad's death
5. **Consistent Text:** Quranic text shows remarkable consistency across manuscripts
6. **Historical Impact:** Transformed Arabian society and created civilization
7. **Contemporary References:** Non-Muslim references to early Islam

### Challenging Evidence:
1. **Late Sources:** Earliest biographies written 100-200 years after Muhammad
2. **Hagiographical Elements:** Biographies contain miraculous elements
3. **Historical Gaps:** Limited contemporary documentation from 7th century Arabia
4. **Textual Variants:** Some Quranic textual variants exist
5. **Historical Criticism:** Questions about traditional narrative from modern scholarship
6. **Moral Questions:** Some actions (military campaigns, marriages) raise ethical questions

### Evidence Quality Assessment:
- **Independent Sources:** Limited contemporary non-Muslim sources
- **Hostile Witnesses:** Some Byzantine and Persian references
- **Temporal Proximity:** Biographies 100-200 years after events
- **Archaeological Corroboration:** Some archaeological support for early Islamic sites
- **Textual Consistency:** High degree of Quranic textual uniformity
