## CLAIM SUMMARY

**Core Claim:** Jesus of Nazareth is the historical instantiation of the Logos (the Word made flesh).

**Historical Assertion:** A specific 1st-century Jewish teacher from Nazareth embodies the personal source of reality.

**Key Implications:**
- The transcendent Logos entered history at a specific time and place
- Jesus' life, death, and resurrection reveal the nature of ultimate reality
- Historical events (crucifixion, resurrection) have cosmic significance
- Christianity provides the definitive revelation of the personal source

---

## EXPLICIT ASSUMPTIONS

1. **Historical Existence:** Jesus of Nazareth existed as a historical figure
2. **Divine Identity:** Jesus claimed and/or was understood to be divine
3. **Resurrection:** Jesus was resurrected from the dead
4. **Apostolic Witness:** The New Testament records reliable testimony about Jesus
5. **Continuity:** The Christian church preserves authentic understanding of Jesus

---

## PRIMARY HISTORICAL ASSERTIONS

1. **Existence:** Jesus lived in 1st-century Palestine under Roman rule
2. **Ministry:** Jesus taught, performed miracles, and gathered followers
3. **Crucifixion:** Jesus was crucified under Pontius Pilate
4. **Resurrection:** Jesus rose from the dead and appeared to followers
5. **Church Foundation:** Jesus' followers established a movement that became Christianity
6. **Scriptural Record:** The New Testament provides reliable historical information

---

## WHAT WOULD FALSIFY THIS

### Direct Falsification:
1. **Non-Existence:** Proof Jesus never existed historically
2. **Contradictory Nature:** Jesus' teachings contradict Logos nature (e.g., advocating evil)
3. **Resurrection Disproof:** Evidence Jesus' body remained in the tomb
4. **Historical Impossibility:** Events described couldn't have occurred as claimed
5. **Apostolic Fraud:** Proof the disciples knowingly fabricated the story

### Indirect Falsification:
1. **Better Explanation:** Alternative account better explains all historical data
2. **Lower Debt Alternative:** Another candidate has strictly lower explanatory debt
3. **Internal Inconsistency:** Christian theology contains irreconcilable contradictions
4. **Failed Predictions:** Christian claims about reality consistently fail
5. **Moral Failure:** Christian practice produces worse outcomes than alternatives

---

## CORRESPONDENCE EVIDENCE

### Supporting Evidence:
1. **Multiple Independent Sources:** Josephus, Tacitus, Pliny (non-Christian references)
2. **Early Manuscripts:** New Testament manuscripts closer to events than other ancient works
3. **Embarrassing Material:** Crucifixion (shameful death) preserved despite embarrassment
4. **Hostile Witness Consistency:** Non-Christian sources confirm basic facts (existence, crucifixion)
5. **Rapid Spread:** Movement spread quickly despite persecution
6. **Martyr Witness:** Early followers died for claims they could have recanted
7. **Empty Tomb Tradition:** Multiple independent traditions about empty tomb
8. **Women Witnesses:** Women as first resurrection witnesses (not credible in 1st century)
9. **Transformational Impact:** Changed lives and societies historically

### Challenging Evidence:
1. **Miraculous Claims:** Resurrection and miracles require supernatural explanation
2. **Gospel Differences:** Variations between Gospel accounts
3. **Pauline Priority:** Paul's writings earlier than Gospels
4. **Historical Gaps:** Limited contemporary non-Christian documentation
5. **Theological Development:** Christology appears to develop over time in NT
6. **Interpretive Diversity:** Multiple Christian traditions with conflicting interpretations

### Evidence Quality Assessment:
- **Independent Sources:** 5+ independent sources for basic facts
- **Hostile Witnesses:** Roman and Jewish sources confirm embarrassing facts
- **Temporal Proximity:** Sources within living memory of events
- **Archaeological Corroboration:** Consistent with 1st-century Palestinian context
- **Embarrassing Testimony:** Multiple elements against authors' interests
