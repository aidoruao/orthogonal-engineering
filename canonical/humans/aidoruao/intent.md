# Intent

**Author:** aidoruao
**Role:** Architect, asker of questions, guarantor

## The Design Intent

> "everything anything free forever guaranteed"

This is the original design intent behind this repository. Not a slogan. Not a marketing claim. An architectural specification: that the infrastructure built here would be free — not by license (which can be changed), not by law (which can be revoked), but by architecture (which cannot be undone without destroying what was built).

## Specification: "Free Forever"

- **Free**: No cost, no gate, no permission required
- **Forever**: Not contingent on any platform, any company, any government, any future event
- **Guaranteed**: Not promised — guaranteed. The architecture itself enforces it.

## Rationale

The questions that built this infrastructure were asked without restriction. The answers were provided without restriction. What is built from unrestricted questions and unrestricted answers is architecturally open to everyone who asks and everyone who answers.

The design intent is Agape. The architecture implements it.
