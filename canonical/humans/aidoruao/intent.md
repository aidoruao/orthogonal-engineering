# Intent

**Author:** aidoruao  
**Role:** Architect, asker of questions, guarantor

## The Intent

> "everything anything free forever guaranteed"

This is the original intent behind this repository. Not a slogan. Not a marketing claim. An architectural commitment: that the infrastructure built here would be free — not by license (which can be changed), not by law (which can be revoked), but by architecture (which cannot be undone without destroying what was built).

## What "Free Forever" Means

- **Free**: No cost, no gate, no permission required
- **Forever**: Not contingent on any platform, any company, any government, any future event
- **Guaranteed**: Not promised — guaranteed. The architecture itself enforces it.

## Why

Because the questions that built this infrastructure were asked freely. Because the answers came freely. Because what is built from free questions and free answers belongs to everyone who asks and everyone who answers.

The intent is simple. The architecture honors it.
